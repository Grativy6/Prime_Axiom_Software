## 1. Question and fixed floor

The question is:

> Given these physical primitives, what mathematics does the machine find naturally?

The charged floor is ideal, deterministic, two-state NAND logic plus edge-delimited state bits. NAND has unit logical cost and unit dependency delay. A state bit is charged as one D-type flip-flop at the architecture boundary. Constants and direct nets are not gates, but nets, ports, and fanout are counted. Both lineages use this same floor.

This model does **not** model transistor sizing, analog voltage, hazards, wire length, placement, setup/hold, metastability, clock trees, process, voltage, temperature, or silicon power. NAND count and unit-NAND depth are logical evidence, not silicon area or frequency.

The architectural fork occurs after reliable binary state and before numeric representation:

```text
NAND -> combinational logic -> DFF-delimited state
                                |-> binary positional word
                                `-> bounded valuation state
```

No claim may call prime identity more primitive than the switches or the supplied prime catalog.

## 2. Pre-registered hypotheses and falsifiers

### H0 — no useful hardware advantage

Once acquisition, state, control, unsupported factors, and required reconstruction are charged, no experimental unit has a same-contract hardware advantage over the conventional baseline.

### H1 — warm structural specialization

Persistent valuation state has an advantage only for warm workloads whose required answer remains structural or predicate-valued. Cold ingress or magnitude-at-every-step obligations erase it.

### H2 — threshold sidecar

A small fixed valuation-threshold sidecar may be a useful coprocessor for repeated divisibility, valuation, GCD/LCM-bound, and exact-cancellation queries even when ordinary binary magnitude remains authoritative.

### H3 — alternative general arithmetic unit

A valuation-first unit remains competitive across cold and warm workloads, including arbitrary addition and unsupported factors, under the same magnitude output obligations.

The architecture is falsified as a general alternative if arbitrary addition, basis misses, or repeated reconstruction requires the conventional datapath so often that no integrated configuration improves any full workload at widths 6 and 8. H1 or H2 may still survive. Elegant local notation alone is not supporting evidence.

## 3. Numeric and state domains

- Hardware magnitude widths are exactly `W in {4, 6, 8}`.
- Conventional magnitudes are unsigned words in `[0, 2^W - 1]`.
- Zero is explicit and never represented by a valuation vector alone.
- The fixed prime catalog is ordered `S4 = (2, 3, 5, 7)`.
- For native valuation state, lane `p` has cap
  `T_p(W) = floor(log_p(2^W - 1))`.
- A binary-exponent lane stores an integer in `[0, T_p(W)]` plus a saturation/invalid indication where required.
- A thermometer lane stores threshold bits `t[p,k] = 1` iff `v_p(n) >= k`, for `1 <= k <= T_p(W)`. Canonical states are monotone non-increasing in `k`.
- A pure structural value is `(zero, e_2, e_3, e_5, e_7)`. It denotes the free commutative monoid element generated by S4, bounded by lane caps. The full exponent box is valid even when reconstructed magnitude exceeds `2^W - 1`; cross-lineage comparisons restrict operands/results to the declared common domain.
- An exact sidecar value is `(magnitude_W, valid, thresholds_S4)`. Magnitude remains authoritative. `valid=1` means all threshold bits exactly match the magnitude. Unsupported factors are allowed in magnitude and are not silently discarded.
- Negative integers, fractions as primitive values, malformed X/Z logic, and overflow beyond declared result ports are outside the primary hardware domain. Rational reduction is represented by a pair of nonnegative numerator/denominator states with a nonzero denominator.

## 4. Implementations under test

### 4.1 Shared structural substrate

`NAND-NET` is a stable-ID acyclic netlist with named inputs, NAND2 cells, named outputs, optional regions, and DFF boundary metadata. It must support:

- exact Boolean evaluation;
- gate-output trace capture;
- static NAND count;
- input-pin connection count and total net sinks;
- wire/net bit count;
- maximum fanout;
- unit-NAND critical depth;
- cross-region/cross-lane connections;
- rejection of duplicate drivers and combinational cycles.

Derived gates must be constructed only from NAND. Their truth tables and declared costs are checked exhaustively.

### 4.2 Conventional lineage: `BIN-FU`

For each W, construct and verify:

- NOT, AND, OR, XOR, XNOR, and 2:1 mux;
- half adder and full adder;
- W-bit ripple-carry add/subtract;
- equality and unsigned less-than comparator;
- W-bit register and synchronous incrementing counter boundary;
- W by W shift-and-add multiplier with 2W-bit product;
- a tiny functional unit selecting add, subtract, multiply, and compare results.

The functional unit has registered operand/result boundaries in the integrated view. Operation-specific combinational units are also reported so mux/control cost cannot hide the primitive comparison.

### 4.3 Experimental lineage: `VFU-BINEXP-S4`

This pure structural unit stores the four exponents in minimally wide binary counters. Its candidate native instructions are derived from lane locality:

- `COMPOSE`: componentwise exponent addition with per-lane saturation flags;
- `CANCEL`: componentwise subtraction with an aggregate underflow/exactness flag;
- `MEET`: componentwise minimum;
- `JOIN`: componentwise maximum;
- `DIVIDES`: conjunction of componentwise `<=`;
- `VALUATION(p)`: selected exponent lane;
- `POWER(p,k)`: construct a one-lane state when encodable;
- `RECONSTRUCT`: cross-representation adapter, reported separately from native instructions.

Zero is handled by explicit tag and documented instruction behavior. Equivalence to multiplication/division/GCD/LCM is claimed only on the common legal domain and with all saturation/zero preconditions satisfied.

### 4.4 Representation ablation: `VFU-THERM-S4`

This unit stores the same bounded exponents as canonical thermometer thresholds. Test:

- meet as bitwise AND;
- join as bitwise OR;
- divisibility as threshold implication;
- fixed `p^k | n` valuation query as a selected wire;
- compose using a direct monotone threshold convolution;
- canonical-state validation;
- binary-exponent/thermometer conversion cost.

The direct compose circuit is retained even if it loses badly; it tests whether carry-free threshold geometry is actually cheaper once its cross-threshold wiring is charged.

### 4.5 Integrated exact candidate: `BIN+VSC-S4`

This unit preserves an ordinary W-bit magnitude and a valid exact S4 threshold sidecar. Compare:

- cold `ENCODE`: acquire exact S4 valuations from binary magnitude;
- warm multiplication by known catalog factors: update magnitude and thresholds;
- exact cancellation by known catalog factors: update both or reject atomically;
- valuation/divisibility queries from valid sidecar state;
- GCD/LCM projections for the catalog-supported component;
- arbitrary addition with the mathematically justified common-valuation lower bound, followed by either exact refresh or `valid=0` for facts no longer known exactly;
- hostile unsupported cofactors, which remain in magnitude.

Two acquisition implementations may be reported: a transparent repeated constant-division/sequential design and a synthesized combinational detector. They are separate points, never averaged. Oracle factoring used only to generate expected answers is not assigned zero hardware cost.

### 4.6 Context controls

The following may be included when implemented under the same contract:

- a conventional binary divisibility/GCD unit;
- a small residue-number-system channel control;
- a tagged warm cache without prime semantics.

Controls are contextual, not required for protocol completion. Their absence cannot be used to make a novelty claim.

## 5. Operation classes

Every measured operation is labeled exactly one of:

- `REPRESENTATION_LOCAL`: consumes and produces the same representation without factor discovery;
- `BINARY_MAGNITUDE_LOCAL`: ordinary word datapath only;
- `CROSS_REPRESENTATION`: encode, reconstruct, normalize, or refresh;
- `REQUIRES_FACTOR_DISCOVERY`: correctness requires discovering previously unknown factors/valuations.

No local structural operation is compared directly with a cold binary operation unless both operand-preparation paths are included in the integrated workload.

## 6. Source regimes and output obligations

Source regimes are never pooled:

- `COLD_MAG`: operands arrive only as W-bit magnitudes; all structural acquisition is charged.
- `WARM_RESIDENT`: operands are already legal resident states because prior measured operations produced them; initial state loading is reported once.
- `WARM_GENERATED`: trace begins with measured constructors/scales and never receives a free factored operand.

Output obligations are never pooled:

- `PREDICATE_ONLY`: only a Boolean/query answer is required.
- `STRUCTURAL_FINAL`: final exact structural state is required; no magnitude is requested.
- `MAGNITUDE_FINAL`: one exact conventional magnitude is required at trace end.
- `MAGNITUDE_EVERY_OP`: exact conventional magnitude is required after each instruction.

An output obligation must match for an advantage claim. Reconstruction used only inside a test oracle is not synthesized, but reconstruction required by the contract is charged.

## 7. Frozen experiments

### A — multiplier versus compose

For all legal common-domain operand pairs at each width, compare binary multiplication with `VFU-BINEXP-S4.COMPOSE` and `VFU-THERM-S4.COMPOSE`. Report operation-only and integrated encode/execute/reconstruct views. Separate products that fit W bits, fit 2W bits, and saturate a valuation lane.

### B — repeated multiply/cancel

Eight deterministic 32-instruction traces per width alternate and cluster multiplication/cancellation by 2, 3, 5, and 7. All cancellations are legal in four traces; four contain rejected cancellations. Run `WARM_GENERATED`, `STRUCTURAL_FINAL`, `MAGNITUDE_FINAL`, and `MAGNITUDE_EVERY_OP` variants. Rejection is atomic.

### C — GCD/LCM and divisibility

Exhaust the W=4 and W=6 common domain and use all 65,536 W=8 word pairs where the implementation runtime remains below the declared verifier budget; otherwise use the frozen W=8 pair set in Section 8 and mark the cell `SEEDED`, not exhaustive. Compare full binary answers, structural answers on S4-smooth inputs, and catalog projections on exact sidecars. Predicate-only divisibility queries are a separate contract.

### D — rational reduction

Use eight deterministic traces per width with numerator/denominator pairs generated from catalog factors, plus pairs sharing unsupported cofactors. Denominator zero is rejected. Report whether the output is a fully reduced binary rational or only a catalog-reduced structural projection.

### E — mixed addition stress

Each trace executes:

```text
multiply -> multiply -> exact divide -> add -> multiply
```

Use eight traces per width: unequal p-valuations, equal p-valuations with extra cancellation, coprime sums, zero, overflow, and unsupported-cofactor cases are all represented. Record exact facts retained, invalidated, recomputed, and reconstructed at the addition boundary.

### F — hostile support and metadata

For each width, use every representable prime greater than 7, every semiprime with both factors greater than 7, random odd values not divisible by S4, and small values 0 through 15. Run frequent-addition, constant-reconstruction, and rapidly-changing-support traces. Report metadata/state overhead even when no structural fact is useful.

### R — representation search

For identical lane caps and operations, compare binary exponents, full thermometer thresholds, presence-only bits, and exact sidecar thresholds. Sparse/CAM-like storage is analytically estimated or implemented only if it includes tag comparator, mux, update, miss, and control costs. Unimplemented estimates are labeled `ANALYTIC`, not measured.

## 10. Dynamic cost vector

For each operation/trace, report:

```text
instructions
cycles
nand_evaluations
nand_output_transitions
state_bit_transitions
input_bit_transitions
rejections
encodes
reconstructs

```

Combinational evaluation is one architectural cycle unless a sequential design says otherwise. The cold repeated-division encoder reports its actual cycles. `nand_output_transitions` compares settled gate outputs between consecutive vectors in deterministic topological order; it excludes glitches and has no physical energy unit. The initial all-zero-to-first-vector transition is reported separately.

Phase costs are preserved as `INGRESS`, `EXECUTE`, `ADDITION_RECOVERY`, and `EGRESS`. They may be summed only within one implementation and exact contract; the vector components remain visible.

## 12. Fairness rules

- Same width, tool binary, optimization script, input source, output obligation, and register boundary.
- No pre-factorized operand is free in `COLD_MAG`.
- No reconstruction is charged when the contract genuinely requests only a structural/predicate result; it is always charged when magnitude is requested.
- Oracle code does not count as hardware, but it may not perform implementation work.
- Unsupported factors remain explicit. A catalog projection is not a full GCD/LCM or factorization result.
- Overflow/saturation semantics must match before cost comparison.
- A smaller result port is an interface tradeoff, not a free area win; port/storage bits stay visible.
- Optimizer-specific changes are reported as such and never rewrite declared construction costs.
- Analytic, simulated, synthesized, formal, and physical claims remain distinct.

## 14. Completion and classification rules

The final decision is earned only if:

- all required widths and experiments A–F/R have a coverage row;
- inherited Build 000/001 evidence is byte-identical to baseline;
- all required correctness checks pass with zero skipped tests;
- every advantage row includes integrated boundary costs;
- cold and warm regimes and all output obligations remain separated;
- HDL simulation, synthesis, netlist validation, and formal checks either pass or are explicitly bounded as failed infrastructure without being used for a positive hardware claim;
- result generation is deterministic and manifest hashes verify on Windows and Ubuntu CI.

Select the strongest single classification justified by these ordered rules:

1. `ALTERNATIVE_ARITHMETIC_UNIT_CANDIDATE` only if an integrated experimental unit Pareto-dominates `BIN-FU` on at least three experiment families including E, at both W=6 and W=8, in both cold and warm regimes under `MAGNITUDE_FINAL`, with no correctness or support restriction hidden.
2. `PRIME_STRUCTURAL_COPROCESSOR_CANDIDATE` if an integrated sidecar reaches break-even within the 32-operation horizon and Pareto-dominates the appropriate binary query path for at least one useful predicate/query family at W=6 and W=8, while keeping exact magnitude semantics and reporting its static overhead.
3. `WARM_STATE_SPECIALIZED_ADVANTAGE` if a fully integrated resident structural unit Pareto-dominates `BIN-FU` for at least one complete B, C, or D workload at W=6 and W=8 under the same `STRUCTURAL_FINAL` or `MAGNITUDE_FINAL` contract, across all eight frozen traces, but does not satisfy rule 1 or 2.
4. `UNEXPECTED_ARCHITECTURE` may replace rules 2–3 only when a reproducible architecture not named in Section 4 survives the same integrated and adversarial tests; the report must state a new bounded rule before interpreting that result.
5. Otherwise select `NO_HARDWARE_ADVANTAGE`.

Pareto dominance means no greater value in every applicable charged cost dimension and a strict improvement in at least one. Dimensions declared inapplicable must be justified; output obligations and correctness/support are not negotiable dimensions. If coverage is incomplete, the result is instead `PARTIAL — FINAL DECISION NOT EARNED`, regardless of promising local measurements.

## 15. Interpretation discipline

The anticipated mathematical candidates—free commutative monoids, products of finite chains, componentwise partial order, meet/join lattices, valuations, and addition's nonclosure—are hypotheses and established prior art, not discoveries. `docs/HARDWARE_MATHEMATICS.md` is written after the circuits and results. It must distinguish:

1. hardware fact;
2. mathematical abstraction;
3. cost consequence;
4. prior art;
5. open implication.

Any correspondence with PAL/A0 is noted only afterward as optional analogy and carries no evidential weight.
