## Implementation boundaries

- `BitState` is the abstract two-state floor. `GateNetwork.Nand` is the only primitive combinational gate; derived circuits must expose NAND count and modeled depth.
- High-level integer types are allowed only at explicit semantic/oracle, ingestion, reconstruction, reporting, or benchmark boundaries. Do not use them inside a gate-level operation while claiming gate-level execution.
- Prime coordinates denote the positive-integer free commutative monoid only under a declared prime basis. Zero, sign, finite-basis escape, exponent overflow, and unverified factor certificates require explicit states.
- Build 003 may use `BigInteger` at its declared software semantic/ingress/reconstruction/reporting boundary. Its ordered trial divider is a transparent bounded factor-discovery implementation, not gate execution, a competitive factorizer, or a succinct formal primality certificate.
- Build 003 derived multiplication receipts require exact reconstruction-verified parents and must cite them. Output factor discovery stays zero; required magnitude reconstruction remains charged. General addition must record ordinary magnitude addition and fresh output discovery.
- Build 003 comparison accounting separates receipt-construction, integrity-replay, and egress reconstructions; the total is their checked sum. Do not restore the old conceptual one-reconstruction shorthand. Addition exponent reads/writes count only input common-factor projection, never freshly discovered output factors or a local addition rule. Mixed-sign addition comparisons remain rejected until an explicit borrow/subtraction trace exists. An exact zero-tag multiplication may short-circuit exponent reads and merges only after input receipt acquisition; receipt issuance, integrity replay, and egress remain charged.
- Build 004 may use `BigInteger` and exact rationals at its declared semantic software boundary. Its work counters are representation-level operation counts, not elapsed-time, energy, gate, area, or universal cost measurements. Keep abstract structure, software execution, and physical-hardware implications independently labeled; Build 004 hardware fields remain `NOT_MEASURED`.
- Derivation DAG nodes are immutable and content-addressed. `JOINT`, `ALTERNATIVE`, and `TRANSFORM` are distinct constructors; canonicalization may reorder commutative parents but must not collapse different topology. Cache projections must be recomputed and checked against the DAG before they can serve as evidence.
- A Build 001 nonzero hybrid value denotes `sign * cofactor * product(bank[i]^exponent[i])`. The cofactor is exact ordinary magnitude. `KnownExact` lanes certify the cofactor is free of that bank prime; `CertifiedLowerBound` lanes permit additional copies in the cofactor while keeping the represented integer exact.
- `Canonical` and `Partial` describe valuation knowledge, not numeric exactness. Never read a lower bound as an exact exponent or silently replace unknown structure by zero. Refresh, normalize, migration, and reconstruction are charged operations.
- Keep bank configuration/validation, ingress, native work, maintenance, and egress separate. The heterogeneous cost-vector fields are not a universal unit and must not be summed with post hoc weights.
- Bank migration is an exact global maintenance operation over affected values, not metadata relabeling. A prime hidden in a magnitude is not adaptively discovered without a charged computation.
- Keep conventional and experimental implementations independently testable. Do not redefine ordinary arithmetic to make an alternative representation pass.
- Build 002 hardware claims stop at ideal NAND2 graphs, DFF boundary counts, settled-vector traces, common-flow synthesis, bounded simulation, and formal miters. They do not imply placed/routed area, frequency, energy, analog behavior, or process-independent optimality.
- Use deterministic exhaustive and seeded randomized differential tests. Add a regression fixture for every discovered failure.
- Failed VM producers invalidate their destination atomically and preserve distinct source registers; if a destination aliases a source, destination invalidation wins. Failed scalar queries clear old scalar output. Never allow prior successful state to survive as the result of a failed instruction.
