## Controlling distinctions retained during review

- PAL is a mechanical ledger, not a mathematical foundation or physical theory.
- Omega remains metalinguistic; no software constructor or computational state in this repository is identified with or derived from it.
- A0 is a supplied cut in a declared account, not a bit, zero, prime, transistor, or globally first physical event.
- Mathematical realizations are removable, bounded implementations; success does not redefine the controlling framework.
- Tests and receipts are evidence with declared ceilings, not adoption or authority.
- The A0/Software document begins from a supplied boundary account and explicitly does not translate Omega into software.

Those points primarily prevented category errors. They did not dictate NAND, binary words, prime bases, operation names, test bounds, results, or the Build 001 recommendation.
