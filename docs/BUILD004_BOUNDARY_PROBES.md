# Build 004 boundary probes

These probes are deliberately small. They expose where exact multiplicative provenance remains useful and where another algebra or an evidence crossing is required.

## Calibration and units

The model has four independent components:

```text
exact or approximate numeric coefficient
SI-like base-dimension exponent vector
typed derivation root
evidence/calibration envelope
```

Ratio-scale gains, dividers, gear ratios, and defined unit conversions compose by multiplying coefficients and adding dimensions. They are the positive case.

Required break cases:

- Celsius: same temperature dimension, affine offset; an explicit conversion transform is required.
- dB: logarithmic scale; an explicit conversion transform is required.
- polynomial or lookup calibration: nonlinear/opaque transform; exponent merge is insufficient.
- correlated uncertainty: two scalar error bars cannot be independently combined without covariance.
- rounded regression coefficient: numeric exactness is approximate even if its decimal string can be factored.
- expired calibration: the mathematics may replay while the evidence envelope is outside its validity interval.

The elementary charge is a useful exact SI-definition fixture. A measured, non-defining quantity is the counterfixture: its reported uncertainty and source status must remain explicit. The difference is source status, not arithmetic difficulty.

Potential correspondence after the test:

- CLEF helps force the observable, resolution/aperture, baseline, noise model, uncertainty, cost channels, stopping rule, and readability wall to be declared.
- BLA can name the residual opened by a failed prediction/calibration check, but cannot infer the cause or authorize a conclusion.
- BRT&AIC can distinguish local source state, relevant boundary, receiver response, recoverable trace, and parent-layer readout.

None replaces SI, UCUM, GUM, instrument specifications, or domain calibration practice.

## Exact combinatorial probability

A hypergeometric point is the strongest clean workload because its terms are exact products and quotients of binomial coefficients. Factorial valuations produce the point receipt without factoring the large result.

A tail or interval event is the deliberate boundary. It sums exact point weights. Prime-exponent addition would multiply probabilities and be wrong. The correct receipt therefore retains an `ALTERNATIVE`/sum node or crosses to canonical exact rational magnitude.

If an observed overlap is later compared with the exact distribution, the arithmetic does not choose the null model, threshold, multiple-testing correction, or causal interpretation. Those belong to a separately sourced statistical/evidence envelope.

## Distributed fusion

The active-source projection is valuable when the query is precisely membership, overlap, or union. It can prevent an observation from being multiplied twice. It does not by itself recover the common likelihood state, identify unmodeled shared process noise, validate conditional independence, or establish source quality.

The test therefore retains both:

- a replaceable, exact source-support cache; and
- the derivation/payload references needed to reconstruct the shared state.

The interesting result is not whether a prime product exists in abstract space; arbitrarily large products are unproblematic as mathematics. The engineering question is whether materializing that product offers anything over its exponent vector or an explicit set. Build 004 establishes exact equivalence and selected structural widths separately; it does not measure materialization cost, query latency, allocation, or hardware cost.

## Database and ETL

The receipt is irreducible relative to the scalar output: two different executions can produce the same value, and one value does not contain enough information to reconstruct which execution occurred. The receipt is therefore a sidecar semantic layer, not an intrinsic property recoverable from arithmetic alone.

Build 004 demonstrates joins and alternatives with a small derivation polynomial/DAG. It also demonstrates that equal source support and multiplicity can hide different pairings. A full ETL system would additionally need schema evolution, row/column identity, query-plan versus semantic equivalence, transactions, nondeterminism, external side effects, retention, redaction, and access control.

## Manufacturing BOM

The Build 004 toy keeps only the useful core: two assemblies with equal computed quantity or cost can retain different supplier, lot, and component derivations; a shared-part query can expose common ancestry. This is a later product surface, not the research target. Real BOM work would require units, tolerances, substitutions, lots, scrap, serials, lifecycle changes, and authority rules.

## Just intonation and sound

An interval such as `3/2` is exact and prime-local. Intervals compose by exponent addition and invert by exponent negation. Cents, nominal Hertz, and PCM are derived readouts.

The WAV renderer records the explicit numerical and render policy. Deterministic byte replay is established only by the external verifier on its recorded host/runtime; no cross-platform floating-point theorem is claimed. A finite sample stream is not the exact rational frequency, and equal pitch class does not imply equal derivation. Phase, waveform, envelope, sample clock, DAC, transducer, room, hearing, and interpretation live beyond the symbolic ratio.

This makes audio a good boundary instrument: the exact receipt stays compact and human-meaningful while the emitted trace honestly records the approximation crossing.

## Structural accumulator and privacy

The transparent accumulator demonstrates only:

- deterministic source-to-coordinate assignment under a validated registry;
- membership by divisibility or bit lookup;
- overlap/union; and
- the size difference between raw product and direct vector/set forms.

It also demonstrates leakage: anyone with the registry and token can test participation. Its mandatory receipt fields are:

```text
security classification = NOT_CRYPTOGRAPHIC
privacy classification  = NO_PRIVACY
integrity digest         = content address only
authenticated commitment = NOT_PROVIDED
membership proof         = NOT_PROVIDED
zero-knowledge proof     = NOT_PROVIDED
```

The user's deeper intuition survives in a bounded form: privacy can be helped structurally by never collecting or routing unnecessary information. It cannot become "unreducible to an algorithm" in an implemented digital system. The enforcement structure, protocol, physical isolation, or cryptographic scheme is itself an algorithmic/physical mechanism with assumptions and failure modes.
